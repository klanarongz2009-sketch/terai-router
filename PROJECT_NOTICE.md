# Project notice

[ภาษาไทย](PROJECT_NOTICE.th.md)

This is an advance notice. TER-AI is an indie project run by one person, so it can stop with little or no warning.

## What can happen

- **Paused for a while (personal reasons):** the maintainer starts school around November 1-2, 2026. Updates, issue replies and releases may slow down or stop for a time.
- **Closed without a stated reason:** the project may close without an explanation.
- **Closed because of a legal claim about models:** some optional models and data used by TER-AI have their own licenses (see the [license table](README.md#license) and [NOTICE](NOTICE)). If there is a legal claim about any of them, the affected part, or the whole project, may be taken down.
- **Closed for good:** in the worst case, the project may be closed permanently.

## What this means for you

- Nothing here is a promise of support, updates or uptime.
- Code you already downloaded keeps its license (MIT for TER-AI code; other parts as listed in [NOTICE](NOTICE)).
- Optional model weights are downloaded from their own hosts, not from this repository, so they may stop being available separately from this project.
- If you depend on TER-AI, keep your own copy of the version you use.
