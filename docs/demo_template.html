TER-AI

Offline Thai chat and typo-tolerant intent router. Runs fully in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere unless you turn on Web search. Made with Sesame.

Off: every answer comes from this page. On: questions the built-in knowledge cannot answer, and questions about current facts (ปัจจุบัน, ล่าสุด, ตอนนี้), are sent to th.wikipedia.org and en.wikipedia.org. Your name, memory and chat history are never sent.
Reads each reply aloud with a Thai (th-TH) voice already on your device, through the browser's Web Speech API. Nothing is downloaded or sent by this page. The Wayu voice of TTH runs only in the Python package (terai tth).
